Shhhcribble — Studio wireframes

Living set — updated in place as iterations continue. Direction: "one timeline, two weights". Dictations are a high-frequency, semi-throwaway log; notes are the durable library; documents are long-form transcripts. Decision record: studio-wireframes.md beside this file.

Last major revision 2026-07-25 (direction locked with Hendri). Rail is five items on every screen; the titlebar always reads Shhhcribble.

1 · Today

Single chronological stream of transcriptions only — notes live in their own tab. Documents anchor as bordered cards; dictations pass through as borderless lines at full-strength text, always fully expanded — no truncation. Every item's content starts on one left edge; the time sits at the trailing edge, permanently. Copy leads the meta line and is always visible; delete sits far right and appears on hover only (third row shown hovered). Chevrons step a day; the day label opens the month popover (screen 8).

4 · Pinned

Importance above, urgency on top. "On your screen" strip = active stickies with Unstick. Below, the cross-type board of pinned notes + documents. Stuck notes are always pinned (default coupling), so they appear in both — the strip manages the screen, the grid indexes importance. Cards are a fixed height and roughly square — a wall of stickies, not a list — each carrying real preview text so the board can be scanned without opening anything. Clicking a card jumps to the item in its home tab.
